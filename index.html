📋 Exam preparation guide — how to get ready for your test
  • Abdominal ultrasound: Fast for 6–8 hours before your slot (water is fine). No smoking or chewing gum.
  • Pelvic / early pregnancy ultrasound: Drink 4–6 glasses of water about an hour before and hold your bladder — a full bladder gives clearer images.
  • Whole abdomen / KUB: Both fasting and full-bladder prep apply.
  • Thyroid, breast, soft tissue, 2D echo: No special preparation needed.
  • Blood tests: Some tests require fasting — confirm with us when booking.
  • Bring your doctor's referral slip if you have one, and a valid ID. Our staff will confirm any specific instructions when we confirm your appointment.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything patients usually ask before their first visit.

Do I need an appointment, or can I just walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome at both branches — booked patients are prioritized. Booking online takes under 2 minutes, or you can reach us by SMS or phone, and we'll confirm your slot by text.

Are you open on weekends?

Yes — both branches are open 7 days a week, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM. Kawit stays open until 5:30 PM on Saturdays.

How much do your exams cost?

Rates depend on the specific exam or procedure. The fastest way to get current pricing is to send us a message on Facebook Messenger or call your nearest branch — we'll reply with the details right away.

Do I need to pay when I book?

No. Booking is free and there's no prepayment — you only pay when you visit the clinic.

How do I prepare for my exam?

Some exams need preparation — for example, abdominal ultrasound requires 6–8 hours of fasting, while pelvic and early-pregnancy scans need a full bladder. See the full preparation guide above; our staff will also confirm any instructions when we confirm your appointment.

Where exactly are you located?

Kawit: A-3 Ground Floor, ASJ Building, Centennial Road, Batong Dalig, Kawit, Cavite.
Imus: Seven One Building, Tanzang Luma V, near Robinsons Imus, Cavite.
